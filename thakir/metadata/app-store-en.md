## Description
Thakir helps you remember more with less effort.

Scan a page from a textbook or your notes; Thakir extracts the text on your device and turns it into flashcards: term & definition, question & answer, and fill-in-the-blank. Review the suggestions, tweak anything, then start studying.

Spaced repetition does the rest: cards you know well come back in days or weeks, cards you forgot come back in minutes.

Features:
• Document scanning with automatic cropping and perspective correction
• On-device text recognition, Arabic and English (Arabic requires iOS 18+)
• Three card types generated automatically, editable before saving
• Review sessions with Again / Hard / Good / Easy grading
• Weekly stats, daily streak and mastery per deck
• No account, no internet, no ads. Your data never leaves your device.
